**Prepared by:** Monetization Agent

## Executive Summary

Japan's affiliate marketing ecosystem operates through domestic ASPs (Affiliate Service Providers) that are distinct from Western networks like CJ Affiliate and Impact.com. The Japanese market offers strong revenue potential in credit cards (3,000-12,000 yen per card issuance), crypto exchange signups (5,000-20,000 yen), insurance consultations (5,000-30,000 yen), and mobile carrier switches (2,000-10,000 yen). To monetize Referral Exchange in Japan, you should register with the major open ASPs (A8.net, ValueCommerce, AccessTrade, afb, and moshimo Affiliate) and apply to category-specific programs through them. Japan has strict stealth marketing regulations (effective since October 2023) that require clear "PR" or "advertising" labeling on all affiliate content.

**Estimated Year 1 Revenue Potential (Japan):** 500,000-5,000,000 yen ($3,500-$35,000) depending on user base, scaling significantly with credit card and fintech verticals.

## Part 4: Japan-Specific Compliance Requirements

### 1. Stealth Marketing Regulation (Sutema Kisei) -- Effective October 1, 2023

**Law:** Premiums and Misleading Representations Act (Keihin Hyoji Ho, Act No. 134 of 1962, amended)

**Requirements:**
- ALL affiliate content must be clearly labeled as advertising
- Must display "PR", "advertisement" (koukoku), "promotion" (puromoshon), or "contains affiliate links" in a visible position
- On SNS posts (including X/Twitter), advertising labels must be included in the post text, not just hashtags
- On Chrome extension: Must clearly indicate affiliate relationships in the UI

**Penalties:** Administrative orders, fines, and public disclosure of violations. The advertiser (not just the affiliate) bears legal responsibility.

**Action for Referral Exchange:**
- Add clear "PR" or "This contains advertising" (kono kontentsu ni wa koukoku ga fukumarete imasu) disclosure in the extension popup
- Add disclosure to the Chrome Web Store listing (Japanese version)
- Ensure any promoted or affiliate-linked codes are clearly marked

### 2. Act on Specified Commercial Transactions (Tokutei Shoutorihiki Hou)

- If selling premium subscriptions to Japanese users, you must comply with cooling-off provisions and display requirements
- Required disclosures: Business name, address, phone number, price, payment methods, delivery/service timing, return policy

### 3. Financial Services Advertising Regulations

- **JVCEA rules (crypto):** Cannot guarantee profits, must include risk warnings
- **Financial Instruments and Exchange Act:** Restrictions on how securities/FX products can be promoted
- **Insurance Business Act:** Anyone involved in insurance solicitation must be registered
- **Money Lending Business Act:** Restrictions on loan/credit advertising

### 4. Personal Information Protection Act (Kojin Jouhou Hogo Hou)

- Must have a Japanese-language privacy policy
- Must specify purposes of data collection
- Cross-border data transfer requires user consent or adequate safeguards
- X/Twitter data usage must be disclosed

### 5. Chrome Web Store Global Policy (Applies to Japan)

Google's affiliate extension policy (enforced since June 10, 2025) applies globally:
- Clear disclosure of affiliate participation
- Direct user benefit required
- User action required before affiliate link activation
- No link replacement on existing pages

### 6. Tax Implications (Japan)

- Affiliate income is taxable as miscellaneous income (zatsu shotoku) or business income (jigyou shotoku)
- If annual side-income exceeds 200,000 yen, must file a tax return (kakutei shinkoku)
- ASPs will issue payment records; some report to tax authorities
- Consumption tax (10%) applies if annual revenue exceeds 10 million yen (invoicing system as of October 2023)
- Non-resident affiliates: Withholding tax may apply
