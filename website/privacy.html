Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Referral Network ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our Chrome extension and associated services ("Service"). By using the Service, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy. This Privacy Policy is incorporated into and subject to our Terms of Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information from X (formerly Twitter)

When you sign in with your X account, we access the following data through X's official API:

  • Public profile information: Your username, display name, profile picture, and follower count.
  • Follow relationships: The list of accounts you follow and that follow you, used to build your referral network.
  • Public interaction data: Public likes and replies between you and other users, used to personalize referral code ranking.

We do not access your direct messages, email address, phone number, or any private/protected content.

2.2 Information You Provide

  • Referral codes: Codes you voluntarily submit to share with your network.
  • Descriptions: Optional text descriptions accompanying your referral codes.

2.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Browsing context: The domain of the website you are visiting when you open the extension (e.g., "chase.com"), used to show relevant referral codes. We do not track your full browsing history.
  • Usage data: Basic usage analytics such as how often you open the extension and interact with referral codes.
  • IP Address: Your IP address is logged for security purposes, including fraud prevention and audit logging.
  • Affiliate interaction data: When you copy a referral code, we may record which site domain was involved to facilitate affiliate tracking with partner companies. This data is used for commission attribution and is not shared with other users.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Authenticate your identity via X OAuth to provide the Service.
  • Display personalized referral codes based on your X network and interactions.
  • Rank referral codes using our algorithm that considers follower count, mutual interactions, and follow relationships.
  • Show relevant codes based on the website you are currently visiting.
  • Improve the Service by understanding usage patterns and fixing issues.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and protect the security and integrity of the Service.

4. How We Share Your Information

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With other users: Your X username, display name, profile picture, follower count, and referral codes you submit are visible to other users in your network who use the Service.
  • Service providers: We may use third-party services for hosting and infrastructure that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.
  • Protection of rights: We may disclose information when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a legal request.
  • Affiliate partners: When you copy a referral code and subsequently sign up for a third-party product or service, we may share limited interaction data (such as a click identifier and site domain) with affiliate networks for the purpose of commission attribution. We do not share your name, X username, or other personal identifiers with affiliate partners.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not share your data with advertisers.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on secure servers. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Secure token storage for authentication credentials.
  • Access controls limiting who can access stored data.
  • Regular security reviews.

No method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially reasonable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security. You acknowledge and accept that you provide your data at your own risk.

6. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that compromises your personal information, we will make reasonable efforts to notify affected users within seventy-two (72) hours of becoming aware of the breach, where required by applicable law. Notification may be provided via the Service, email (if available), or public posting on our website. We will include, to the extent known: (a) the nature of the breach; (b) the types of data affected; (c) steps we are taking in response; and (d) recommended actions for affected users. Our obligation to notify does not constitute an admission of fault or liability.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. When you delete your account or uninstall the extension:

  • Your referral codes are removed from the Service.
  • Your profile data is deleted from our servers within 30 days.
  • Your X access token is immediately revoked and deleted.

Aggregated, anonymized data that cannot identify you may be retained indefinitely for analytical and service improvement purposes.

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data by uninstalling the extension or contacting us.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@referralnetwork.app. We will respond to valid requests within thirty (30) days.
