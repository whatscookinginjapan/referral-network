## Detailed Description
Referral Network is a Chrome extension that helps you share and discover referral codes from people you trust.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Sign in with your X (Twitter) account
2. Browse any of our 60+ supported sites (Chase, Amex, Capital One, Coinbase, Airbnb, and more)
3. See referral codes ranked by popularity, trust, and freshness
4. Share your own referral codes with your network

FEATURES
- 60+ supported sites across 16 categories: credit cards, banks, fintech, investing, crypto, shopping, travel, food delivery, insurance, health & fitness, education, VPN & security, telecom, cloud & tech, energy, and subscriptions
- Smart ranking: codes ranked by popularity (copy count), verification status, user trust, and freshness
- Auto-detect card type: paste a Chase or Amex referral link and the card type is automatically identified
- Search and filter: find specific card types (e.g., Sapphire Preferred, IHG)
- Code verification: submitted codes go through safety screening
- Report suspicious codes: community-driven trust system
- Suggest new sites: request support for additional referral programs

PRIVACY & TRUST
- We only access your public X profile for authentication
- We do NOT access your DMs, email, or private content
- Referral codes are verified for safety before display
- Full privacy policy: https://whatscookinginjapan.github.io/referral-network/privacy.html

DISCLOSURE
The person sharing a referral code may receive a reward if you use it. Referral Network may earn a commission from affiliate partnerships.
